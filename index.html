Fall 2023

Advanced Musical Concepts

Fall 2023 marked a transition into advanced musical concepts. In Theory III and Musicianship III, I moved beyond basic diatonic harmony into chromaticism and complex forms, which sharpened my analytical ears—a crucial skill for any producer. I balanced this academic rigor with active performance, taking Applied Music to refine my individual technique and College Choir to maintain my ensemble skills.

Class List

  • MUS A280: Theory 3
  • MUS A281: Musicianship 3
  • MUS A290: Applied Music
  • MUS A120: College Choir

Skills Learned

  • Chromatic Harmony: Analyzing secondary dominants and modulation (essential for understanding complex song structures).
  • Advanced Sight-Reading: Quickly interpreting difficult melodic passages.
  • Ensemble Blend: Listening critically to fit my voice within a larger mix of sound.
  • Solo Performance: Managing stage presence and preparation through private instruction.

Spring 2024

Completing the Foundation

This semester represented the completion of my core music foundation. I finished the theory sequence with Theory 4 and Musicianship 4, which likely introduced 20th-century techniques and atonal concepts, challenging me to understand "noise" and dissonance in new ways. Continuing with Applied Music and College Choir ensured that my performance abilities stayed sharp right before I transferred to my upper-division coursework.

Class List

  • MUS A285: Theory 4
  • MUS A286: Musicianship 4
  • MUS A290: Applied Music
  • MUS A120: College Choir

Skills Learned

  • 20th Century Analysis: Understanding modes, synthetic scales, and non-functional harmony.
  • Complex Rhythm Dictation: Transcribing mixed meters and polyrhythms by ear.
  • Repertoire Development: Building a portfolio of polished solo pieces.
  • Musical Discipline: The consistency required to complete a four-semester sequential track.

Fall 2024

Introduction to the Professional Audio World

This semester served as my introduction to the professional audio world. I focused heavily on the physics of sound and the logic behind the software I use daily. In Introduction to Recording, I learned the crucial "rules" of the studio, while Audio Programming taught me how to build plugins from scratch and how to use game audio in Unity. Simultaneously, Design for the Internet I gave me the tools to start building my online presence, ensuring I could showcase the music I was learning to create in Composition Laboratory.

Class List

  • MUSIC162: Introduction to Recording and Sound Reinforcement
  • MUSIC496: Introduction to Audio Programming
  • ART344: Design for the Internet I
  • MUSIC207: Composition Laboratory
  • MUSIC106A: Introduction to Aural Skills I
  • MUSIC170: University Chorus

Skills Learned

  • Signal Flow & Gain Staging: Mastering the path of audio from source to destination.
  • Basic Web Development: Using Figma + Webflow and CSS to build a basic website.
  • Critical Listening: Identifying frequencies and intervals by ear.
  • Logic-Based Thinking: Applying algorithmic thinking to audio tasks.

Spring 2025

Expanding the Palette

Spring 2025 was about expanding my palette and getting comfortable with experimentation. Intermediate Recording moved me beyond the basics into complex mixing techniques, while Electro-Acoustic Music Composition I forced me to treat sound as a malleable object rather than just a recording. I also balanced this intense technical work with Yoga and Mindfulness, which surprisingly became a key tool for maintaining focus during long mixing sessions. My historical knowledge deepened in Music History II, giving context to the styles I was studying.

Class List

  • MUSIC262: Intermediate Recording and Sound Reinforcement
  • MUSIC260: Electro-Acoustic Music Composition I
  • MUSIC205B: Comprehensive Musicianship
  • MUSIC308B: Music History II: Classical and Romantic
  • MUSIC106B: Introduction to Aural Skills II
  • MUSIC291: Yoga and Mindfulness for the Musician
  • MUSIC530: Music Internship

Skills Learned

  • Microphone Placement: Stereo techniques (XY, ORTF) and instrument-specific miking.
  • Sound Synthesis: Creating textures using oscillators and envelopes rather than presets.
  • DAW Automation: Using automation for creative effect rather than just volume control.
  • Session Focus: Techniques to reduce ear fatigue and maintain creative flow.

Fall 2025

Coding Meets Art

This semester was a deep dive into the intersection of coding and art. I returned to programming in Introduction to Audio Programming (Advanced), where I began building my own tools, and pushed the boundaries of texture in Electro-Acoustic Music Composition II. I also began looking toward life after graduation with Professional Orientation for Music Performers, which helped me strategize my career path. The Music Internship kept me grounded in the real-world industry while I explored abstract concepts in the classroom.

  • MUSIC515: Professional Orientation for Music Performers
  • MUSIC305A: Comprehensive Musicianship

Spring 2026

Professional Application and Advanced Synthesis

I am currently in the thick of my final semester, where my focus has shifted entirely to professional application and advanced synthesis. A major highlight to kick off the term was being invited to attend the NAMM show, which gave me an incredible look into the future of music technology. In the studio, I am solidifying my workflow on the Neve console, both through my coursework in The Art of Recording and my current role as a Neve Intern. On the creative side, Music and Visual Media has me coding custom devices in Max for Live, while Advanced Sound Design for Film is sharpening my post-production chops. Crucially, I am also crossing the finish line on my academic core: I am wrapping up my final music theory requirements in Comprehensive Musicianship and taking my coding skills to the next level in Design for the Internet II, moving beyond basics into advanced, interactive web development. I'm rounding out this heavy workload by studying the Romantic era in Music History II and performing in University Chorus.
