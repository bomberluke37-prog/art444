Overview

I'm Luke Schaben, a Music Recording Technology and Audio Design (MRTAD) undergraduate at San Diego State University, graduating in 2026. This site is a portfolio of my work spanning studio recording, sound design, audio post-production, and web design — built throughout my time in the program.

What is MRTAD?

Music Recording Technology and Audio Design is an interdisciplinary program at SDSU that combines music theory, studio recording, audio engineering, sound design, and music technology into one degree.

Students work hands-on in professional recording studios on campus, learning everything from microphone technique and signal flow to mixing, mastering, and audio post-production for film and media.

The program bridges the gap between creative musicianship and technical audio production, preparing graduates for careers in recording studios, live sound, broadcast, game audio, and the music industry at large.

What are your goals after graduation?

After graduating in 2026, I'm looking to break into the professional audio world — whether that's working in a recording studio, doing audio post-production for film and TV, or pursuing sound design for games and interactive media.

I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of music production and technology. I'd love to work with artists and producers to help shape the sonic identity of their projects, while continuing to develop my own skills as an engineer and designer.
